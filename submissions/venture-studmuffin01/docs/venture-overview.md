## Opportunity

Artificial intelligence tools such as Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity are rapidly becoming part of everyday work. However, many professionals struggle to achieve consistent, high-quality results because they lack practical training in how to communicate effectively with AI.

Common challenges include:

- Receiving responses that are too generic or incomplete
- Spending excessive time revising AI-generated outputs
- Uncertainty about how to structure prompts effectively
- Difficulty integrating AI tools into daily workflows

As AI adoption accelerates across industries, there is growing demand for accessible, practical learning solutions that help individuals and organizations maximize productivity and value from these tools.

---

## Solution

AI Prompting Academy is a workplace learning platform that helps professionals develop practical AI prompting skills through structured frameworks, guided lessons, and real-world exercises.

The Academy launched with its first course, **Prompt Like a Pro: The SCORE Method for Microsoft Copilot**, a framework-based learning experience that teaches users how to generate more effective responses from Microsoft Copilot by:

- Defining context clearly
- Specifying objectives effectively
- Establishing constraints and expectations
- Improving response relevance and quality
- Reducing time spent revising outputs

The long-term vision is to expand the Academy with additional frameworks and learning pathways tailored to leading AI platforms and workplace use cases.
